Task 5.5: DevTools & Color Justification

Inspected via Chrome DevTools and documented color design choices.

Why I Chose These Colours:

  1. Readability & Contrast: I chose a soft off-white background (#f1f5f9) and dark charcoal text (#334155) to reduce eye strain and maintain high contrast.
  2. Visual Hierarchy: The deep navy blue (#1e293b) header clearly grounds the page, making the main content stand out immediately.
  3. Modern Accent: Royal blue (#2563eb) was picked for subheadings to give a clean, modern, and professional tech vibe.
